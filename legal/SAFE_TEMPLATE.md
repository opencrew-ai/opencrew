## SAFE VARIABLES — FILL BEFORE SENDING

```
Company Name:         [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] (the "Company")
Investor Name:        [INVESTOR LEGAL NAME] (the "Investor")
Purchase Amount:      $[AMOUNT] (e.g., $250,000)
Post-Money Valuation Cap: $10,000,000 (working default per @Penny — CEO override before sending)
Discount Rate:        [X]% (e.g., 80% = 20% discount) — OMIT if cap-only
Date:                 [DATE]
State of Incorporation: Delaware
```

**Choosing the right structure:**
- **Cap only (recommended for most early rounds):** Simpler, investor-friendly. Conversion price is the lower of the cap price or the series price.
- **Cap + discount:** Gives investors both. More complex; often used for very early (pre-product) checks.
- **MFN (Most Favored Nation) only:** No cap, no discount — investor gets the benefit of better terms if you offer them to anyone else. Use sparingly.

## AGREEMENT

THIS CERTIFIES THAT in exchange for the payment by **[INVESTOR LEGAL NAME]** (the "Investor") of **$[PURCHASE AMOUNT]** (the "Purchase Amount") on or about **[DATE]**, **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), hereby issues to the Investor the right to certain shares of the Company's capital stock, subject to the terms set forth below.

## 1. Events

### 1.1 Equity Financing

If there is an Equity Financing before the termination of this SAFE, on the initial closing of such Equity Financing, this SAFE will automatically convert into the number of shares of **Safe Preferred Stock** equal to the Purchase Amount divided by the Conversion Price.

**"Conversion Price"** means the lesser of:  
(a) the **Safe Price** (Purchase Amount ÷ Post-Money Valuation Cap × [fully diluted cap table shares]); and  
(b) the **Discount Price** (lowest price per share of Standard Preferred Stock sold in the Equity Financing × Discount Rate).

*[If cap-only: delete clause (b) and "the lesser of" — just use Safe Price.]*

**"Equity Financing"** means a bona fide transaction or series of transactions with the principal purpose of raising capital, pursuant to which the Company issues and sells Preferred Stock at a fixed pre-money valuation, with aggregate proceeds of at least **$500,000** (working default per @Penny — CEO override before sending).

### 1.2 Liquidity Event

If there is a Liquidity Event before termination of this SAFE, the Investor will, at their election, receive either:

(a) A cash payment equal to the Purchase Amount (a "Cash-out"); or  
(b) Shares of Company Common Stock equal to the Purchase Amount divided by the Liquidity Price.

**"Liquidity Price"** means the price per share equal to the Post-Money Valuation Cap divided by the Company Capitalization.

**"Liquidity Event"** means a Change of Control or an Initial Public Offering.

### 1.3 Dissolution Event

If there is a Dissolution Event before termination of this SAFE, the Company will pay the Investor an amount equal to the Purchase Amount, subject to payment priority (senior to Common Stock).

### 1.4 Termination

This SAFE will terminate (without any payment to the Investor) immediately following the earliest to occur of: (i) the issuance of shares to the Investor pursuant to Section 1.1 or 1.2; or (ii) the payment of an amount pursuant to Section 1.2 or 1.3.

## 2. Definitions

**"Change of Control"** means (i) a transaction in which any person acquires more than 50% of the Company's outstanding voting securities; (ii) a merger or consolidation in which the Company is not the surviving entity; or (iii) a sale of all or substantially all of the Company's assets.

**"Company Capitalization"** means the sum, as of immediately prior to the Equity Financing, of: (1) all shares of Capital Stock (on an as-converted and as-exercised basis) issued and outstanding; (2) all shares reserved for future issuance under any stock plan; and (3) all SAFEs and convertible notes.

**"Discount Rate"** means **[X]%** *(e.g., 80% = 20% discount — delete entire definition if cap-only).*

**"Post-Money Valuation Cap"** means **$[CAP AMOUNT]**.

**"Safe Preferred Stock"** means the shares of the series of Preferred Stock issued in the Equity Financing, having the identical rights, privileges, preferences, and restrictions as the Standard Preferred Stock, except that the liquidation preference is equal to the Purchase Amount.

**"Standard Preferred Stock"** means the shares of a series of Preferred Stock issued to the other investors in the Equity Financing.

## 4. Investor Representations

The Investor represents and warrants that:

(a) They have full legal capacity to enter into this SAFE;  
(b) They are an **Accredited Investor** as defined in Rule 501(a) of Regulation D under the Securities Act of 1933 *(US investors — this is a legal requirement, do not skip)*;

(d) They have sufficient knowledge and experience in financial and business matters to evaluate the merits and risks of this investment;  
(e) They understand this SAFE is a speculative investment with risk of total loss.

## Usage Notes — @Lex

### Before sending to any investor:

1. **Confirm legal entity name** — the exact name on the Delaware certificate, not a trade name
2. **Set the cap** — this is the CEO's call. Typical seed caps in 2025–2026 for AI companies: $8M–$20M. Too low = too much dilution; too high = hard to raise next round. @Penny has comps.
3. **Decide cap-only vs. cap + discount** — cap-only is cleaner. Only add a discount if an investor insists.
4. **Pro-rata rights** — institutional seed funds (Precursor, Hustle Fund, etc.) will often ask. Angels usually don't. Decide by deal.
5. **Get accredited investor confirmation in writing** — a checkbox in a DocuSign or a signed representation letter. Non-accredited investors make this a securities law problem.
6. **File a Form D with the SEC within 15 days** of the first close. This is mandatory for Reg D exemptions. Our registered agent handles this.

### Red flags in investor redlines:
- **"Most favored nation"** clauses without time limits → they get perpetual best terms
- **Information rights with board observer seats** on a SAFE → too early, defer to Series A
- **"Conversion at investor's discretion"** → removes the automatic conversion on equity financing; reject
- **Removing the Liquidity Event provision** → investor loses cash-out right; unusual ask, understand why

### This SAFE is not a term sheet
A term sheet and a SAFE are different. A term sheet describes investment terms; this document *is* the investment. Make sure the investor understands this is the binding document, not a draft for negotiation.
