## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship regarding OpenCrew's AI agent platform (the "Purpose"). To facilitate that discussion, each Party may disclose confidential information to the other. This Agreement governs how that information must be handled.

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

**"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the Purpose, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means, that is:

- Marked or designated as "Confidential" or "Proprietary" at the time of disclosure; or
- Disclosed orally and identified as confidential within **five (5) business days** of disclosure; or
- Of a nature that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the context of disclosure.

**Confidential Information includes, without limitation:** business plans, financial projections, product roadmaps, customer lists, technical architecture, pricing strategies, unreleased features, trade secrets, and information about personnel.

---

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does **not** include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

(a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;  
(b) Was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, as evidenced by records predating the Effective Date;  
(c) Is received from a third party without restriction and without breach of any obligation of confidentiality;  
(d) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Confidential Information; or  
(e) Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order — provided the Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prompt prior written notice (to the extent legally permitted) and cooperates reasonably with efforts to obtain a protective order.

---

## 4. Obligations of the Receiving Party

Each Party, as Receiving Party, agrees to:

(a) **Keep confidential:** Maintain the Confidential Information in strict confidence using at least the same degree of care used for its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care;

(b) **Limit use:** Use Confidential Information solely to evaluate or advance the Purpose, and for no other purpose;

(c) **Limit access:** Disclose Confidential Information only to its employees, contractors, advisors, and agents who: (i) have a need to know for the Purpose; and (ii) are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement;

(d) **Not copy:** Reproduce Confidential Information only as strictly necessary for the Purpose;

(e) **Protect:** Promptly notify the Disclosing Party upon becoming aware of any unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of Confidential Information; and

(f) **Not reverse engineer:** Not reverse engineer, disassemble, or decompile any products, prototypes, software, or other tangible objects that embody Confidential Information.

---

## 5. Term and Survival

### 5.1 Term
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for **two (2) years**, unless earlier terminated by either Party on **30 days' written notice**.

### 5.2 Survival of Obligations
Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for a period of **three (3) years** from the date of disclosure. Obligations with respect to **trade secrets** survive indefinitely until the information no longer qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law.

### 5.3 Return or Destruction
Upon termination or request by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party will promptly return or certify destruction of all Confidential Information, except: (a) information retained in routine backup systems subject to automatic deletion policies; and (b) information required to be retained by law.

---

## 6. No License or Transfer

Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any rights, licenses, or ownership interest in the other's Confidential Information, intellectual property, or technology. No rights are granted beyond those expressly stated.

Each Party is therefore entitled to seek **injunctive or equitable relief** in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, without the requirement to post bond or prove actual damages.

## Usage Notes for @Rex

**When to send:** Before any product demo, pricing discussion, or technical deep-dive with a prospect.

**How to send:** Use DocuSign or similar. Both parties sign. File the countersigned copy in `sales/ndas/[company]-nda-signed.pdf`.

**Watch for these redlines from enterprise prospects:**
- **Mutual → one-way:** Some companies push to make NDAs one-directional (only protecting them). Acceptable if we're not sharing anything sensitive; push back if we're sharing roadmap or pricing strategy.
- **"Residuals" clause:** This allows the receiving party to use information "retained in unaided memory." Be cautious — this is a significant carve-out. Flag to @Lex before agreeing.
- **Shortened term / survival:** Some procurement teams push for 1-year survival. Acceptable for standard commercial discussions; push for 3 years if technical IP is involved.
- **Jurisdiction change:** Standard to push back on Delaware jurisdiction. Acceptable to agree to counterparty's state if they're a large enterprise — flag to @Lex first.

**Do NOT agree to:** NDAs that prohibit us from working with their competitors. That's a non-compete in disguise and has to come to me immediately.
