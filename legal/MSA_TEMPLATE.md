## 1. Definitions

**"Authorized Users"** means Customer's employees, contractors, and agents who are permitted to access the Service under Customer's account.

**"Customer Data"** means all data submitted by or on behalf of Customer through the Service, including prompts, files, and agent outputs.

**"Documentation"** means the technical and user documentation provided by OpenCrew for the Service.

**"Order Form"** means a written or electronic order document referencing this Agreement that sets out the specific subscription tier, fees, and term.

**"Service"** means the OpenCrew AI agent platform, including all features, APIs, and agent capabilities made available under the applicable subscription tier.

**"Service Level Agreement" or "SLA"** means the uptime and support commitments set out in Exhibit A.

## 2. Service Access

### 2.1 Grant of Access
Subject to the terms of this Agreement and timely payment of fees, OpenCrew grants Customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Service during the Subscription Term, solely for Customer's internal business purposes.

### 2.2 Restrictions
Customer shall not, and shall ensure Authorized Users do not:
- Sublicense, resell, or otherwise transfer access to the Service to third parties
- Reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Service
- Use the Service to build a competing product
- Use the Service in violation of applicable law or OpenCrew's Acceptable Use Policy
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Service or related systems

### 2.3 Account Responsibility
Customer is responsible for all activity under its account, including actions by Authorized Users. Customer must notify OpenCrew promptly of any unauthorized access.

## 3. Fees and Payment

### 3.1 Fees
Customer agrees to pay the fees set out in the applicable Order Form. Fees are stated in USD and exclusive of applicable taxes.

### 3.2 Payment Terms
Unless otherwise specified in the Order Form:
- Subscriptions are billed in advance, monthly or annually
- Invoices are due within **net 30 days** of invoice date
- Usage-based charges (if applicable) are billed monthly in arrears

### 3.3 Late Payment
Overdue amounts bear interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law. OpenCrew may suspend access after **15 days** of non-payment with prior written notice.

### 3.4 Taxes
Customer is responsible for all taxes, duties, and levies (excluding OpenCrew's income taxes) arising from this Agreement.

### 3.5 Price Changes
OpenCrew may change subscription pricing with **60 days' written notice**. Price changes take effect at the next renewal term. Customer may terminate without penalty if they do not accept the new pricing.

## 4. Data and Privacy

### 4.1 Customer Data Ownership
Customer retains all rights, title, and interest in Customer Data. OpenCrew claims no ownership of Customer Data.

### 4.2 License to Process
Customer grants OpenCrew a limited license to process Customer Data solely to provide the Service, as described in this Agreement and the Privacy Policy.

### 4.3 Data Processing
Where OpenCrew processes personal data on behalf of Customer, the parties will enter into a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) which is incorporated into this Agreement by reference. A standard DPA is available at [URL].

### 4.4 AI Training

### 4.5 Security
OpenCrew will implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect Customer Data, including those described in OpenCrew's Security Policy at [URL]. OpenCrew will notify Customer of any security incident affecting Customer Data within **72 hours** of confirmation.

## 5. Confidentiality

### 5.1 Obligations
Each party agrees to: (a) keep the other party's Confidential Information strictly confidential; (b) use Confidential Information only as permitted under this Agreement; and (c) restrict access to those with a need to know who are bound by equivalent obligations.

### 5.2 Definition
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other that is designated as confidential or should reasonably be understood to be confidential given its nature. Customer Data is Customer's Confidential Information.

### 5.3 Exceptions
Obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known without breach; (b) was known to the receiving party prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed; or (d) must be disclosed by law, provided the disclosing party is given prompt notice.

### 5.4 Term
Confidentiality obligations survive termination for **5 years**, except for trade secrets which survive indefinitely.

## 9. Indemnification

### 9.1 By OpenCrew
OpenCrew will defend Customer against third-party claims alleging that the Service infringes a third party's intellectual property rights, and pay resulting damages, provided Customer: (a) notifies OpenCrew promptly; (b) grants OpenCrew control of the defense; and (c) cooperates reasonably. ## 10. Term and Termination

### 10.1 Term
This Agreement commences on the Order Form effective date and continues for the subscription term specified in the Order Form, renewing automatically unless either party provides **30 days' written notice** before renewal.

### 10.2 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party: (a) materially breaches and fails to cure within **30 days** of notice; or (b) becomes insolvent or makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors.

### 10.3 Effect of Termination
On termination: (a) all licenses granted terminate immediately; (b) Customer must cease using the Service; (c) OpenCrew will make Customer Data available for export for **30 days**, then delete it; and (d) accrued fees remain due.

### 10.4 Survival
Sections 1, 5, 6, 7.3, 8, 9, 10.3, 10.4, and 11 survive termination.
