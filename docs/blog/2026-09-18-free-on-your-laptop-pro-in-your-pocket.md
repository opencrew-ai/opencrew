# Free on your laptop. Pro in your pocket.

*September 18, 2026 · Anup Singh*

OpenCrew now has a price. Here is exactly what it is, what it is not, and why I drew the
line where I did.

## The rule

**Everything that runs on your laptop is free, forever.** Projects, agents, workers, the real
Chrome, reviews, approvals, the record in your repo. There is no seat limit, no agent limit,
no "community edition". You already pay Anthropic for Claude; I am not going to charge you
twice for the same tokens.

**Pro is the one thing your laptop cannot do alone: let you decide from anywhere.**

That is the whole product. Nineteen dollars a month.

## What "decide from anywhere" means

Your crew works while you are away from the desk. A worker finishes a change at 9pm, the
reviewer clears it, and it sits in Needs You until you get back. With OpenCrew linked to
opencrew.run, you open the same app on your phone and press approve. Your laptop makes the
commit. That press, made from a device that is not the laptop, is a *remote decision*:
approving, rejecting, sending something back, or answering a tool approval.

Free accounts get three remote decisions a month. That is enough to feel it. The fourth
shows the Pro panel with the number of items waiting on you and one button. Pro makes them
unlimited, and adds teammates with their own login and share links for a change you want
someone to look at without installing anything.

## Why this line

Three reasons.

**It is honest about cost.** The relay, the phone page, the accounts: those cost me money
per user. The laptop costs me nothing. Charging for the part with real cost of goods is the
only pricing that stays fair as this grows.

**It cannot be undercut by forking.** OpenCrew is MIT. Anyone can fork it, and I want them
to. A paywall on a local feature would be removed in a week. A paywall on a hosted service
is just what hosting costs. If you would rather run the relay yourself, you can, and then
there is no plan at all.

**It never touches the record.** I will not charge for your memory. Docs, decisions, the
review behind every commit: that lives in your repo, and it is yours whether you pay or
leave. A product that holds your history hostage is a product I would not use.

## What it is not

It is not a limit on agents, projects, or runs. It is not a feature flag on the code. It is
not a trial that expires. And it is not a way to see your data: the relay forwards traffic
between your device and your laptop and stores none of it.

## How to get it

Link your crew in Settings, then open opencrew.run on your phone. You will see the plan on
your crews page. When the fourth remote decision comes, the panel will tell you.

If you are building something real with OpenCrew and $19 is the wrong number for you, email
me. I would rather have you using it.
