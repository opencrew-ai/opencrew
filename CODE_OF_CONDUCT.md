# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want OpenCrew's community to be welcoming to everyone, regardless of background, identity,
or experience level. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for all.

## Our standards

**Do:** be kind and constructive; assume good intent; give and accept feedback gracefully;
focus on what's best for the project and community.

**Don't:** harass, insult, or demean others; publish someone's private information; use
sexualized language or imagery; troll or derail discussions.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/DSpbp4Fn7e) or via a GitHub issue marked confidential in its
title. All reports are reviewed; maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or ban
contributors whose behavior violates this code.
